Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

CT Phenology (“the app”) is a free educational app for exploring the seasonal timing of plants and animals. This policy explains what information the app uses and who receives it. In short: the app has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics, and it does not sell or share your data for marketing.

Information we use

Location (optional)

The app asks for your device’s location only if you tap “Use GPS” on the Walk screen. Location is used while the app is open, never in the background. You can decline, and the app still works using a default point in Connecticut.

When you share your location, your approximate coordinates are sent directly from your device to the third-party services listed below, so they can return nearby weather and sightings. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your location.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your preferences (such as your selected state) and, if you use the “I saw this today” feature, a personal log of species you recorded. This stays on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or photos. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.

Third-party services

To work, the app requests data from the services below. When your device contacts them, they receive standard technical information such as your IP address, and, if you chose “Use GPS”, the coordinates in the request. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

  • The Weather Company (api.weather.com): weather forecasts and growing degree days for your location.
  • iNaturalist (api.inaturalist.org): recent nearby observations and species photos.

When you tap a link such as “Log on iNaturalist”, you leave the app and that site’s own policies apply.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings, and you can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Material changes will be noted in the app’s store listing or release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email us.
